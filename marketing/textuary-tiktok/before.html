Why quiet technology helps us think

Good tools create space for attention instead of competing for it.

By Alex Morgan · 18 August 2026 · 8 min read · Share · Save · Comment

Reading on the web should feel simple. Yet useful words often arrive surrounded by menus, promotions, alerts and recommendations all asking to be noticed at once.

A calmer page changes the relationship between the reader and the article. The typography becomes comfortable, the measure becomes generous and the story is allowed to set its own pace.

Quiet design is not empty design. It keeps the controls people need and gives everything else permission to step back.
